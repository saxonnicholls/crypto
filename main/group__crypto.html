Fail to compile unless snicholls-crypto is at least major.minor.patch.

Put one in a header of yours that depends on a crypto feature, and a build against an older crypto stops with a message that names the number rather than deep inside a template. No trailing semicolon in the expansion: write SNICHOLLS_CRYPTO_REQUIRE_VERSION(0, 1, 0); like any declaration.

Encode a version as one integer the preprocessor can compare.

Two decimal digits each for minor and patch, which is plenty and keeps the number readable: 1.0.0 is 10000, 1.2.3 is 10203.

◆ errc

enum class snicholls::crypto::errc : unsigned char
strong

Why an operation failed. The values are stable: new ones are only ever appended.

Enumerator
invalid_length 

An input had the wrong number of bytes.

invalid_private_key 

A private key or scalar was zero or not below the group order.

invalid_public_key 

A public key was badly encoded, not on the curve, or the point at infinity.

invalid_signature 

A signature was out of range, badly encoded, or did not verify.

invalid_recovery_id 

A recovery id was not 0 to 3 (or an Ethereum v not 27 or 28).

high_s 

A signature's s was above n / 2 where only low-S is accepted.

recovery_failed 

No public key could be recovered: r is not the x of a curve point, or Q is infinity.

small_order 

X25519 gave the all-zero shared secret: the peer's key has small order.

invalid_seed 

A seed was not 16 to 64 bytes.

invalid_derivation 

A BIP-32 child is invalid (I_L at or above n, or a zero key): use the next index.

hardened_required 

A non-hardened index where only hardened derivation is defined (SLIP-10 Ed25519, or from a public key).

invalid_path 

A derivation path did not parse.

invalid_mnemonic_length 

A mnemonic was not 12, 15, 18, 21 or 24 words, or entropy not 16 to 32 bytes in steps of 4.

unknown_word 

A mnemonic word is not in the word list.

invalid_checksum 

A mnemonic's checksum, or an extended key's, did not match.

non_canonical 

Text was not in the canonical form this library accepts (see the function's documentation).

invalid_extended_key 

A serialised BIP-32 extended key was malformed or inconsistent.

invalid_character 

Text held a character outside its alphabet (base58, bech32), or outside printable ASCII.

invalid_hrp 

A bech32 human-readable part was empty, too long, out of range, missing, or not the one expected.

invalid_witness_version 

A segwit witness version above 16, or the wrong checksum variant for the version.

invalid_witness_program 

A segwit witness program outside 2 to 40 bytes, or not 20 or 32 bytes for version 0.
