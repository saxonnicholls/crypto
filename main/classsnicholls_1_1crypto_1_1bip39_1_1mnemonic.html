Detailed Description

A valid BIP-39 mnemonic: 12 to 24 word indices whose checksum holds. Secret; wiped on destruction.

Overview

Made only by from_entropy and parse, so every value has a correct checksum. Holds word indices, not text; to_text renders the text.

Guarantees and contracts

Move-only (clone() copies), noexcept, constexpr; the indices are wiped on destruction and on move.
